Paper's ramp now falls smoothly from its cards (50) to its text (950), with the same spacing as Ink, instead of a big jump between the 900 and the 950. The 100–900 steps move darker, so in Paper the secondary fill (Secondary buttons and badges, Progress tracks, Skeletons, Kbd, avatar fallbacks) has a little more definition, and the muted and subtle text colours read stronger: muted from 5.3:1 to 7.0:1 on white, subtle from 3.9:1 to 4.5:1. The 50 and the 950 are unchanged, and so are Graphite and Ink. If you use `--paper-100` … `--paper-900` directly, they hold new values.
