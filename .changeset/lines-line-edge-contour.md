"@waveso/ui": minor

Lines now have one token per job, at three strengths of the theme's text colour: `line` (5%) divides (separators, table rows, hairlines), `edge` (10%) bounds a surface (cards, dialogs, menus, popovers, toasts), and `contour` (15%) outlines a control or a region (outline buttons, badges, toggles, the slider thumb, menubars). Every component now uses them. Surfaces (Card, Dialog, AlertDialog, Menu, ContextMenu, Popover, PreviewCard, Select, Combobox, Autocomplete, Toast, ActionBar, the floating Sidebar) now draw their edge as a 1px `border border-edge` instead of a ring: a ring sat outside the surface, past a menu's glass, so it flickered where it crossed text, and Windows high contrast mode drops rings, which left Select, Combobox and Autocomplete lists and the floating Sidebar with no edge at all. Rings are now kept for focus and invalid halos, and a focused control in a selected Table row takes the same focus halo as anywhere else (its white ring is gone). A surface sized by its content looks the same; one given a width (a Dialog, a Card filling a grid cell) is 1px slimmer per side, and popups sit 1px further from their trigger. The floating Sidebar and ActionBar edges move from 5% to the 10% `edge` like other surfaces (ActionBar's extra inner border is gone), and SidebarMenuButton's `outline` variant takes the 15% `contour` like the outline Button.

**Breaking:** `contour` moves from 5% to 15%. If you used `border-contour` or `bg-contour` for hairlines and separators, switch to `border-line` / `bg-line` to keep the 5% look. If you used `ring-1 ring-contrast/10` around surfaces, `border border-edge` is the same colour, drawn inside the surface. If you used `border-shade` for outlines, `border-contour` is the same 15% (the `shade` fill itself is renamed `neutral`, and `highlight` is renamed `highlighted`; see the fills notes).
